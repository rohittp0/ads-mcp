## What I do

- Gather a structured ad plan: campaign objective, targeting, budget, schedule, and placements
- Define the creative requirements (formats, copy, CTA, assets)
- Generate images and videos using the veo3 MCP based on the approved creative briefs
- Prepare Meta ad objects (campaign, ad set, ad, creative) using meta-ads-mcp
- Ask for final approval before uploading assets and creating ads

## When to use me

Use this when you need to create Meta ads end to end and want AI-generated creatives.
Ask clarifying questions if the campaign plan is incomplete or approval criteria are unclear.
